Measuring urban livability in St. Louis using AI and community insights

In this study, we (1) measured urban livability using AI-assisted streetscape audits (N=7,848 street segments); (2) explored the distribution of livability features to identify and understand spatial inequities; and (3) captured community and stakeholder perspectives on how these inequities are produced and experienced, and perceptions of livability.
