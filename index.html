Spatial inequities and livability in St. Louis

A vision–language model audited 7,848 street segments of the City of St. Louis for micro-scale built-environment features. This dashboard puts those results beside the tract demographics they track, and beside what residents and stakeholders said about the same streets.

Favarão Leão AL, Wang Y, Banda BF, Balogun M, Xing E, Gudapati S, Rios-Hernandez M, Jacobs N, Reis RS. Journal of Urban Health, 2026
